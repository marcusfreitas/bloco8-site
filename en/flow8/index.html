Flow8

Your training. Your rules. A strength training log built to record fast without breaking your rhythm.

  • Complete a set with one tap, duplicate the previous one or adjust load and reps on the spot.
  • Load in kg or lb per set, kept in the unit you logged it in.
  • Rest timer that keeps running in the background.
  • History by date or by exercise, weekly volume per muscle group and body measurement charts.
  • Your data stays on your device: no account, no login, works offline.
In development. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.
